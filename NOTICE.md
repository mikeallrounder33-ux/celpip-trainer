# Notice

**Not affiliated with CELPIP.** CELPIP is a registered trademark of Paragon Testing
Enterprises. This project is an independent, unofficial practice tool and has no
connection to, endorsement from, or affiliation with Paragon Testing Enterprises or any
official test administrator.

**No official test material is included.** Every practice item in the built-in bank is
original work written for this project. Items produced by a language model at runtime are
generated fresh and are likewise not drawn from real test material. The project does not
reproduce, paraphrase, or reconstruct any real CELPIP test item, official sample answer,
or third-party published practice item.

**Scores are estimates, not results.** The band levels this tool reports — for Listening
and Reading from a raw-score mapping, and for Writing and Speaking from an automated
rater — are estimates for practice purposes only. They are not official CELPIP results,
they are not produced by a certified rater, and they should not be relied on to predict
an official outcome.

**Your data stays with you.** Practice history is stored in your own browser's
localStorage. Nothing is transmitted to the maintainers of this project. If you configure
a third-party model provider, your responses are sent to that provider under their terms;
if you use the in-browser model, nothing leaves your machine at all.
