CELPIP·Trainer
Not affiliated with CELPIP. CELPIP is a registered trademark of Paragon Testing Enterprises. This is an independent, unofficial practice tool. It contains no official test material — every practice item is original — and the band levels it reports are estimates for practice only, not official results.
Your data stays in your browser. Practice history is saved to this browser's local storage. Nothing is sent to us, and there is no account, no tracking and no analytics. If you enter an API key it is stored locally and sent only to the provider you choose. Use Settings → Export JSON to keep a copy.
