The overall band is then lowest-anchored — pulled toward your weakest dimension rather than averaged, because that is how the real scale behaves. Four dimensions of 6, 7, 8 and 6 give you a 6, not a 7.

The test itself

Built to the official format, including the parts that are unpleasant

LISTENING · 38 QUESTIONS · 55 MIN

The audio plays once

Six parts. No replay button, no transcript until review, and a different voice and accent for every speaker. You cannot go back to a section you have answered.

READING · 38 QUESTIONS · 56 MIN

Four parts, diagrams and all

Correspondence, applying a diagram, information matching and viewpoints. Marked instantly with the correct answers and why each one is right.

WRITING · 2 TASKS · 53 MIN

No spellcheck, and a gate before you submit

Autocorrect is off, as on the real test. Submit early and it stops you once. Then a 90-second proofread you cannot skip.

SPEAKING · 8 TASKS · ~20 MIN

Recording starts whether you are ready or not

Preparation and recording advance on their own, with the same instruction screens between tasks. No pause, no re-record, no skipping.

Unscored trial questions are mixed in. The real test embeds questions that do not count and you cannot identify. Practising without them means you have never had to hit a strange question and keep your nerve. This is the only practice platform I am aware of that reproduces it.
