Practise CELPIP-General under real exam conditions

All four modules, exam-accurate timers, marked in your browser. Free, open source, no account, and nothing about you is collected.

Start practising →

Opens straight into the app. Nothing to install or sign up for.

Listening — 38 questions, 55 minutes

Six parts. Audio plays once, with a different voice and accent per speaker, no replay button, and no transcript until the review screen.

Reading — 38 questions, 56 minutes

Four parts including diagram-matching and gap-fill. Marked instantly with an estimated band, the correct answers, and why each one is right.

Writing — two tasks, 53 minutes

Live word count, spellcheck disabled as on the real test, a forced proofread gate, and a detector for memorised phrases like “I hope this email finds you well”.

Speaking — 8 tasks, ~15 minutes

Preparation and recording start automatically. No pause, no re-record, no skipping. Your speech is transcribed and scored, and you can play your recording back.

Built around the things that actually cost marks

  • Time-use tracker — submit with a fifth of your time left and it stops you once and tells you to fix three things.
  • Template detector — counts memorised scaffolding, penalises the density, and offers natural replacements.
  • Recurring error log — every mistake is tracked across sessions and the top five surface as “your repeat offenders”.
  • Register checker — flags a gendered salutation when the recipient’s gender is unknown, contractions in a formal email, and a missing sign-off.
  • Dashboard — band trend per module, average time used, and which task types you have avoided.

Free, with no key required

Twenty Listening passages, twenty Reading passages, twenty Writing prompts, twenty-eight Speaking prompts and forty-eight reference answers ship inside the page. Listening and Reading mark themselves, and Writing and Speaking are scored, with no model and no internet connection.

For a model rewrite and richer feedback, the app can run an open-weights model on your own device through WebGPU — no key, no account, and nothing leaves your machine. It also works with Anthropic, OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama or LM Studio if you prefer.

Your privacy

There is no account, no sign-in, no cookies, no analytics and no server that stores anything. Your practice history is written to your own browser's local storage and never leaves it, which also means clearing your browser data deletes it — use Settings → Export JSON to keep a copy. If you choose to enter an API key it is stored locally in your browser and sent only to the provider you selected. If you use the in-browser model, nothing is transmitted anywhere at all.
